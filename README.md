# LabourLink — Classical Management Operational System & Social Innovation

An operational platform blueprint applying Henri Fayol's Classical 5 Functions of Management (`Planning`, `Organising`, `Staffing`, `Directing`, `Controlling`) to eliminate wage exploitation and bring structured, direct opportunity to urban informal daily-wage labor markets.

## Core Architecture
1. **Planning**: Diagnostic socio-economic scoping, 6-stage strategic roadmap, and structured urban pilot deployment.
2. **Organising**: 4-stage linear matching workflow eliminating intermediaries — verified profile inception, geo-spatial matching, bilateral agreement, and direct settlement.
3. **Staffing**: Grassroots community coordinators stationed at civic nodal points for assisted digital onboarding and document verification.
4. **Directing**: Behavioral facilitation, multilingual empathy, and employer mutual accountability alignment.
5. **Controlling**: ₹10 Lakh variance budget framework, escrow-backed wage settlement guarantees, and tripartite governance equilibrium (Worker · Employer · Society).
